PhD Candidate · Northwestern University

Amy Krefman

I am a PhD candidate in Health and Biomedical Informatics at Northwestern University, advised by Theresa Walunas, PhD, FACMI, FAMIA. I have over a decade of experience in health research, spanning epidemiology, biostatistics, clinical trials, and health informatics. My current research focuses on building robust, reproducible methods and metrics for computable phenotyping and patient representation.
